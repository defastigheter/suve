3. Data We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data depending on your interaction with us:

4. Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed to enable us to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide support

  • Improve our website and services

5. Legal Basis

The processing of personal data is based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • To perform a contract or take steps prior to entering into a contract
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • Legitimate interest, where the processing is necessary for our legitimate business purposes
  • Your explicit consent (e.g., for newsletters)

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of the processing or as required by applicable law. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymized.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties except when necessary to fulfill contractual obligations or legal requirements. For example, we may share data with trusted partners for IT operations, service, or accounting. All such partners are bound by confidentiality and process data only according to our instructions.

8. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (“the right to be forgotten”)
  • Object to processing or request restrictions
  • Request data portability

If you wish to exercise your rights, you can contact us via e-mail at info@suve.se.

9. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience, analyze traffic, and customize content. You can change your cookie settings at any time in your browser. Read more in our separate Cookie Policy (if available).

10. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and accidental deletion. Only authorized personnel have access to the data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as necessary to reflect changes in legislation or in our business operations. The latest version is always available on this page.
