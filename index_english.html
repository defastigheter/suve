Our Products

Leak Detection Equipment

Our leak detection systems are suitable for a wide range of applications, most commonly used for leak testing headlight housings and fuel tanks.

Winches

Winches are used across a wide range of industrial applications, from lifting to pulling. We supply reliable winches designed for demanding environments and tailored customer specifications.

RBV Climate Unit

The RBV climate unit is specifically engineered for climate control in the driver cabins of the Roslagsbanan railway trains.

LKD Electronics Cooler

The LKD electronics cooler is a mobile cooling system optimized for the F16 fighter aircraft, ensuring stable and reliable cooling of onboard electronics when the aircraft is parked on the ground.

Heat Pumps

As a global leader in climate solutions, Daikin delivers highly energy-efficient heat pumps of exceptional quality. The wide range—including air-to-air, air-to-water, and geothermal systems—is designed specifically for Nordic climate conditions and ensures high performance even in extremely cold temperatures (down to –25 °C / –30 °C depending on model).

About Us

SUVE Energy Products AB is your local expert in climate and energy solutions, with a strong heritage dating back to 1980. From our base in Husum, just north of Örnsköldsvik, we have supplied reliable and customized solutions to households and companies across northern Sweden and the rest of the country for more than four decades.
